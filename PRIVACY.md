# Nexus Hub privacy policy

_Last updated: November 10, 2026_

Nexus Hub is an iPhone app for talking to AI models through **your own computer**. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app and the Nexus Gateway program that runs on your computer.

**In short:** we don't run servers that receive your chats. Your conversations go from your iPhone to your own computer and are stored there. If you connect an online AI service, your messages to it also go to that service. We have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Who we are

Nexus Hub is published by Luis Ibarra. Contact: **support@swiftserviceapp.com**.

## Your chats, agents and attachments

- When you send a message, photo or file, it goes from your iPhone to **Nexus Gateway on your own computer**. The gateway passes it to the AI model you chose and saves the conversation on that computer.
- The connection runs through your **Tailscale** network and is encrypted with HTTPS. It doesn't pass through any server of ours.
- We never receive, see or store your chats, agents, photos or files.
- To delete your conversations, delete them in the app, or uninstall Nexus Gateway and choose to delete its data.

### Memory, agent profiles and usage

These features arrive with the next Nexus Gateway release.

- **Memory notes.** An agent with memory switched on keeps short notes, which you or the agent save. They are stored on your computer, in Nexus Gateway's data folder.
  - The notes are added to the messages sent to that agent's model, so an agent that uses an online service (such as Claude) sends its notes to that service with each message.
  - You can view and delete notes in the app (**Agents › Memory**), or with `/forget` in the agent's chat.
- **Agent profiles** are text files on your computer, sent to the agent's model like any other instructions.
- **Usage records.** For `/usage`, the gateway keeps a log on your computer of each Claude request: the model, token counts and number of web searches. It holds no message text and is never sent anywhere.

AI replies are generated by the models and services **you** choose. We don't filter, review or control them.

## Information that passes through other companies

Some features use services run by other companies, each covered by its own privacy policy.

- **Online AI services you connect** (optional). If you add a service such as Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, OpenRouter or Groq with your own key, the messages, files and conversation history you send to agents using that service go to it, and its privacy policy and terms apply. The same goes for an agent server you connect, such as Hermes Agent on another of your computers. Your keys are stored only on your computer, in Nexus Gateway's data folder, and are never sent to the iPhone.

- **Web search** (optional, arrives with the next Nexus Gateway release). When an agent with web search switched on decides to look something up, the search words it chose leave your computer:
  - **Claude** searches through Anthropic, as part of your Claude request; Anthropic's privacy policy applies.
  - **Local models** search through a SearXNG server that you set up and choose. SearXNG passes the search words to the search engines it is configured to use (such as Google or Brave), under their privacy policies. We don't run that server and never see your searches.
  - The rest of your conversation is not sent to the search engines.

- **Tailscale** (tailscale.com) connects your iPhone and computer. You sign in to Tailscale with your own account. Tailscale sees which of your devices connect to each other, but not the content of your chats.
- **HTTPS certificates.** Nexus Gateway gets a free certificate from Let's Encrypt through Tailscale. This publishes your computer's Tailscale name (for example `my-pc.tailnet-name.ts.net`) in public certificate logs. Nothing on the computer becomes reachable from the internet.
- **Notifications** (optional). If you allow notifications, your computer sends "your reply is ready" alerts through **Expo's push service** and **Apple Push Notification service**.
  - By default an alert only says which agent replied.
  - If you switch on **Show reply text**, the start of the reply also passes through those services.
  - Your phone's push token is stored on your own computer.
- **App updates.** The app downloads updates from **Expo** (expo.dev). Like any download, this shares your IP address, app version and device type with Expo.
- **Speech.** Voice conversations use **Apple's speech recognition**. Depending on your iPhone and language, Apple may process your speech on its servers under Apple's privacy policy. Replies are read aloud by voices built into iOS.

## Information stored on your iPhone

- Your gateway's address and settings are stored in the app.
- The access token for your gateway is stored in the **iOS Keychain**.
- **Face ID.** If you turn on the Face ID lock, iOS does the check. The app never receives your face data.
- **Camera.** The camera is used only when you scan a pairing code or take a photo to send to your agents.
- **Microphone.** The microphone is used only while you are talking in the Voice tab.

## Reporting a reply

If you report a reply, the app opens your email app with that reply filled in, addressed to support@swiftserviceapp.com. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. We use what you send only to look into your report, and you can ask us to delete it.

## Children

Nexus Hub is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
